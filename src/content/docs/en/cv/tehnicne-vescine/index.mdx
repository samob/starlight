### Transition to Open-Source Systems

Of course, I'm not just a "terminal boy"—those who claim everything can be done in text mode are like those who use a hammer to solve all problems. I'm not in that category. I'm old enough to know what the transition was like from Commodore 64 to today's 16 threads / 32 cores with 128GB of RAM and 24TB RAIDZ-3 disk array "systems". One was the realization of a teenager's dreams who grew up in the analog era—the other is today's "gaming consumer grade" design that the same generations encounter. All these systems enable running graphical applications, and this is still my primary way of using and interacting with the computer. That's why in the past I spent considerable time making the transition from previous "Windows Install" thinking to a different process and approach in open-source systems. A particularly big challenge for me was the successful GUI install on FreeBSD 13.1, which I fully documented in a YouTube video.
